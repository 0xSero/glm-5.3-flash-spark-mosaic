## Mission

Minimize mean KL (nats, lower bound) of GLM-5.3-Flash served on ONE DGX Spark (GB10, 121 GB
admissible) via SGLang with the exl3_plain overlay, for an EXL3 3.05bpw artifact with 25% of
routed experts pruned (uniform keep-216 of 288, MoE layers 3..44), using the REAP observations to
rank which experts to keep. Quality is measured against the BF16 teacher on the frozen panel
(65,504 WikiText2 positions) plus 20 pre-registered task samples. The unpruned 2.05bpw point (A0)
is the reference, not the deliverable; the deliverable is the best 25%-pruned 3.05bpw point.
